Connect Gemini

Panel Forge uses Google's Gemini to read pictures, plan panels and draw the art. You need your own API key. It's free to start.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Press Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and press Test key.

Your key is saved only on this computer and is sent only to Google. Picture generation can cost money on paid Gemini plans; check your usage in AI Studio. Privacy policy
