Panel Forge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

The short version

Panel Forge has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking. Your projects stay on your own computer. Pictures and text are sent only to Google's Gemini service, using an API key you provide, when you ask the app to read, plan, draw or edit something.

What is stored on your device

  • Your projects, characters and pictures, saved in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on this computer.
  • Your Gemini API key and model settings, saved in your browser's local storage on this computer.

The developer of Panel Forge cannot see any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

What is sent to Google

When you press a button that uses Gemini (for example "Read this picture", "Plan it for me", "Draw versions", "Apply change" or "Write Veo prompts"), the app sends the relevant pictures and text directly from your computer to the Google Gemini API at generativelanguage.googleapis.com, together with your API key. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Photos of people

Only upload photos of real people when you have their permission. The app shows a reminder when a picture looks like a photo of a real person.

Children

Panel Forge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact
